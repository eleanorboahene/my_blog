Schools in Accra

The school we will talk about today is North Hills International School'

My school is called North Hills International School. It is in Accra, Ghana.

I like my school because the teachers are kind and they help us learn new things. We study Math, English, Science, and French.

Sometimes we also learn about Ghana and the world.

The classrooms are bright and we have computers to use.

There are fun days too, like Sports Day and Carols Day. On Sports Day we run races and play football. On Carols Day we sing Christmas songs and dress up.

My favorite thing is that the classes are small, so the teacher knows all of us.

I feel happy to go to school every morning.

North Hills is the best school because we learn, play, and make friends.
